07 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need good photos for this to work?
Phone photos are fine — most of our early testers shoot everything on a phone in daylight. MenuBoost plans posts around the photos you have and tells you which 3–4 shots would fill the gaps.
Will the captions sound like my restaurant, or like a robot?
You pick a tone (warm family spot, modern cafe, playful street food) and MenuBoost writes in it consistently. Every caption is editable before it goes anywhere.
Which platforms are supported?
Content is formatted for Facebook, Instagram (feed, stories, reels) and TikTok scripts. At launch you copy posts out with one tap; direct auto-publishing is on the roadmap.
Does it handle Thai holidays and local events?
Yes — the calendar is holiday-aware for your region. Songkran, Loy Krathong, Mother's Day and long weekends get themed campaigns planned weeks in advance.
What happens to the menu and photos I upload?
They're used only to generate your content, never shared or used to train public models. In this demo page specifically, nothing is uploaded at all — the flow is simulated in your browser.
When can I get access?
We're onboarding food businesses from the waitlist in small weekly batches so every kitchen gets set up properly. Join the list and we'll email you when your spot opens.
