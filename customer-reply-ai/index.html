07 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Will the AI sound robotic to my customers?

No. Replies are short, warm and written the way you'd write them — you can review the tone and edit any saved answer. Customers see fast, friendly answers, not corporate boilerplate.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It never makes things up. If a question falls outside your business info, it politely tells the customer it's checking, flags the conversation, and notifies you to take over — exactly like the demo above.

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM and LINE Official Account at launch. All three share one knowledge base, so you only maintain your info once.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live in under 10 minutes: connect a channel, paste your FAQ or menu, and turn the assistant on. No developers or coding required.

Does it speak Thai?

Yes — it replies in the customer's language, including Thai, English and mixed Thai-English chat that's common on LINE.

Can I turn it off or take over anytime?

Always. You can pause the AI per conversation or globally, and jump into any chat yourself. When you reply manually, the AI stays quiet on that thread.
