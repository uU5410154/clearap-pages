Frequently asked questions

What accuracy do you guarantee?
Measured on your first 500 real invoices: you see the per-field scorecard and set the auto-post threshold. We don't quote a universal accuracy number — it depends on your invoice mix.
Do we need IT or an implementation partner?
No. Setup is self-serve: connect your inbox, import open POs from a CSV, review your first batch the same day. Human help is included in early access.
Which ERPs do you integrate with?
Today: journal / CSV export any ERP can import. API connectors ship in order of early-access demand — QuickBooks Online and Xero first, then NetSuite and Dynamics 365 Business Central. We won't claim a connector before it exists.
Where do you get my POs and receipts?
A nightly export of open POs and goods receipts from your ERP — templates provided, ~10 minutes to set up. Every match shows the file's freshness; changed or closed POs flag stale.
Is this replacing my team?
No. It removes keying and chasing, not judgment. Partners redeploy the hours into vendor recs, discount capture and close work.
Who owns the match tolerances, and what happens to exceptions?
Yours — per vendor or category, role-restricted, logged. Exceptions land queued with variance computed and evidence attached. Within-tolerance PPV posts to your mapped variance account, never silently into inventory.
How does the accrual export work at close?
Captured-but-unposted invoices export as Dr expense / Cr accrued liabilities, auto-reversing next period. Capture-on-arrival means the accrual reflects what exists, not what someone remembered to forward. GR/IR aging covers the other side of cut-off.
How is our data secured and controlled?
Encrypted in transit and at rest, processed per-customer, never used to train shared models. SOC 2 Type I in progress — report under NDA when issued. This demo uploads nothing.
