Q2 2026 revenue closed at $12.4M, $200K (+1.6%) ahead of budget and +14.6% year-over-year (Q2 2025: $10.8M). June was the strongest month of the half at $4.27M against a $4.15M budget, driven by continued wholesale expansion and a full quarter of the two regional distribution partners onboarded in March.

Gross margin reached 41.8%, 0.9 points above the 40.9% plan. The renegotiated inbound freight contract (effective April) and a richer mix of direct-to-consumer orders more than offset input-cost inflation in apparel, adding $193K of gross profit versus budget.

EBITDA finished at $2.10M, $74K (−3.4%) below the $2.17M plan. The gross-profit beat was more than offset by operating spend: sales & marketing ran $178K over budget as two trade shows were pulled forward from Q3 and paid-acquisition costs rose, while G&A and R&D ran $52K and $37K over, respectively. Full-year EBITDA guidance is unchanged, with the trade-show timing expected to reverse in Q3.

07 — FAQ

Questions finance people ask

Is my financial data secure?
Security is the product's first requirement: encryption in transit and at rest, region-pinned storage, and your data is never used to train models. Note: this page is an early-access preview — the demo above runs entirely in your browser and never uploads anything.
Which file formats are supported?
.xlsx, .xls, and .csv — including multi-sheet workbooks with a P&L, budget, and prior-year tabs. Google Sheets import is on the early-access roadmap.
What if my spreadsheet is messy?
BoardBrief proposes a column and line-item mapping and shows you exactly what it detected before generating anything. You confirm or adjust the mapping once; it's remembered for next month's file.
Can I edit the generated commentary and slides?
Yes. Commentary is fully editable before export, and the PowerPoint you download is a normal, editable .pptx in your own template — not a flattened image.
Does it connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or NetSuite?
Direct connectors are on the roadmap and early-access members vote on the order. Today the workflow is upload-based, which also means it works with any ERP you can export from.
What does early access include?
Founding pricing locked in for 12 months, weekly onboarding calls, and a direct line to the team building the product. We onboard new teams from the waitlist every week.
