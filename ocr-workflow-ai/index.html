01 — Problem

Document automation shouldn't need a dev team

Every company runs on documents. Almost every company processes them the hard way.

Every workflow needs a developer

Automating a single document type means custom code, sprints and a backlog. By the time it ships, the process has changed.

Manual re-keying never dies

Invoices, claims and forms still get retyped from PDF into spreadsheets and ERPs — slow, expensive and error-prone.

Documents live in ten systems

Email, shared drives, the ERP, the accounting tool, Slack threads. Nothing connects, so people become the integration layer.

Automation projects take months

Classic RPA and IDP tools demand consultants and long implementations. Small process changes turn into change requests.

02 — Solution

From custom code to drag-and-drop

Docuflow is Zapier + OCR + AI, purpose-built for business documents. Your ops team builds the automation — visually.

07 — FAQ

      
Frequently asked questions

    

    

      

        What kinds of documents can Docuflow handle? 
        
Anything paper-shaped: invoices, purchase orders, receipts, bank statements, contracts, insurance claims, medical forms, resumes. If it arrives as a PDF, scan or photo, the OCR + AI Extraction blocks can read it — including mixed layouts from many different senders.

      

      

        Do I really need zero engineering help? 
        
For most workflows, yes — you drag blocks and configure them with plain-language settings. The API block exists for teams that want to push data into custom systems; that's the only place a developer might get involved, and even then it's a webhook, not a project.

      

      

        How accurate is the AI extraction? 
        
Accuracy depends on your documents, which is why every workflow can include Validation and Approval blocks: the AI flags anything it isn't confident about and routes it to a person instead of guessing. You get automation for the easy 90% and human judgment for the rest.

      

      

        Which systems does it connect to? 
        
Out of the box: databases, email, ERP systems, Slack and Excel, plus a generic API/webhook block for everything else. During early access we prioritize integrations based on what waitlist members ask for — tell us yours when you sign up.

      

      

        Is my document data secure? 
        
Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, processed only to run your workflows, and never used to train shared models. Enterprise plans add SSO, role-based access and private-cloud deployment for regulated industries.

      

      

        When can I get access? 
        
We're in early access and onboard new teams from the waitlist every week, prioritizing by use case. Join the list, tell us which documents you process, and we'll reach out to schedule your onboarding.
